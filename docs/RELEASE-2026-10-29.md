# Taskmatch commercial launch — 29 October 2026

Scope: explicit provider routing and current priced model catalog, independently
durable task execution, Sega human ownership, prepaid EUR mission quotes and 10%
itemized platform fees. Accepted historical quotes are preserved. Recovery is
operator-selected; there is no historical mass replay.

are healthy; nginx readiness and the rebuilt Next.js service pass. The live
